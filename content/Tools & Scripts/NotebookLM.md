NotebookLM is Google’s AI-powered “research assistant”. For this, you upload source data to NotebookLM.

## Features

| Feature Category            | What You Can Do                                                                |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Summarization & Q&A**     | Upload sources + get summaries, answers with citations                         |
| **Audio Overviews**         | Listen to podcasts or join the conversation interactively                      |
| **Mind Maps**               | Visually explore and navigate your content structure                           |
| **Interface & Notes**       | Manage sources, save notes, pin AI responses, navigate easily                  |
| **Creative Outputs**        | Generate study guides, outlines, FAQs, timelines, briefings                    |
| **Sharing & Collaboration** | Publish notebooks, and work with expert-curated templates                      |
| **Plus Tier Advantages**    | Larger limits, advanced customization, team collaboration, enterprise security |
